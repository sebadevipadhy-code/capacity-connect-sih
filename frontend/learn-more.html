UNDERSTAND THE PLATFORM

What is Capacity Connect?

One platform. One learning ecosystem.

Capacity Connect is a digital capacity building and Learning Management Portal designed to support organizations in developing people, improving competencies and sharing knowledge.

Instead of managing training, assessments, learning resources and progress across different systems, Capacity Connect provides a centralized environment where learners, trainers and administrators can work together.
